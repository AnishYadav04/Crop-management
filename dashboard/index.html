Teacher demo — change a sensor and watch the system respond
  1. Open the esp32 folder in VS Code and start Wokwi. If the board has just restarted, run python scripts/upload_wokwi.py from the project root using the project environment.
  2. Click the circuit background and press F to fit it on screen. The top middle knob is shallow soil moisture; the knob below it is deep moisture. The two knobs on the right control rain and leaf wetness.
  3. Click the shallow-moisture knob and turn it down. The default dry setting is about 20%; about 50% is a useful normal demonstration and 90% demonstrates excess moisture. The simulation index is not a calibrated soil measurement.
  4. Choose Wokwi sensors → Shallow soil moisture → Every reading below. Readings arrive about every 10 seconds; the page refreshes every 10 seconds. Use Refresh readings anytime.
  5. Keep the value below the configured low limit or above the high limit for three readings spanning at least 20 seconds. Allow another 15 seconds for the monitor. Review the suggestion in Actions for your plants.
  6. Click the DHT22 to change air temperature or humidity. A temperature change moves the graph; it does not trigger a dry-soil alert. A wet-canopy demo requires humidity ≥85% and leaf wetness ≥60% for three readings.
  7. Open Ask crop agent and ask “Why is this action suggested?” Record your inspection, then mark the alert resolved. An unresolved alert is not duplicated; the same alert also has a one-hour repeat limit. Use the opposite moisture scenario for a second demo.

These actions suggest inspection; they do not operate a pump or diagnose disease. Weather continues updating independently when Wokwi stops.

Alert history and guidance

An alert records a condition detected by the system. Research adds an explanation and reference links. “Mark reviewed” means you have seen it; “Record what you did” saves your inspection; “Resolve” closes the alert. None of these buttons waters a plant.
